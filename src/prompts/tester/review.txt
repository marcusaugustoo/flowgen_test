Instruction:

- Test: [test description]

  Actual: [actual result]
  Likely cause: [brief analysis]

SUGGESTIONS:
- [Actionable suggestions for the developer]

Context:
Problem: {problem}

Code:
{code}

Test Results:
{test_results}
